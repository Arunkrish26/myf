SMART MONEY HABIT

Why follow the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple starting framework for giving every part of your income a job.

50%

Needs

Essential spending such as housing, food, utilities, transport and medical needs.

30%

Wants

Optional lifestyle spending such as entertainment, shopping, travel and dining out.

20%

Savings

Money for goals, investing, emergency reserves and longer-term financial security.

Why does it help?

The percentages are a guide, not a law. The benefit is that they give you a benchmark for checking whether your spending matches your priorities.
