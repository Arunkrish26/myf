MYFINANCE HELP

Common questions

Quick answers to common MyFinance questions.

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple budgeting guide using 50% for Needs, 30% for Wants and 20% for Savings.

Can the same category be both a Need and a Want?

Yes. MyFinance classifies the individual transaction. For example, Metro to work can be a Need while Metro to meet a friend can be a Want.

Why do I need to choose Need or Want?

A category alone cannot know the purpose of a purchase. Your transaction-level choice makes the budget calculation more accurate.

How are goal contributions handled?

A goal contribution increases savings and goal progress without being automatically counted as another expense.

How do goal contributions affect my transactions?

Goal contributions are stored separately in goal contributions. They increase goal progress and savings tracking without automatically creating a second expense transaction.

Are categories private?

System categories are shared defaults. User-created categories belong only to the logged-in user.

Can I attach a receipt?

Yes. Transaction attachments will be stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket.

Are my transactions private?

User-owned records are protected with Supabase Row Level Security.
